If you click a link to Instagram, WhatsApp or Gmail, those services may set their own cookies on their own websites.

If this ever changes, this section will be updated, and any non-essential cookie will be used only after you agree to it.

7. Accessibility statement

I want everyone, including people with disabilities, to be able to use this website. It is built with the goal of meeting Israeli Standard SI 5568 (based on the international guidelines WCAG 2.0, level AA), as required by the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations, 2013.

7.1 What has been done

  • The whole website can be used with a keyboard only, with a visible focus outline and a “Skip to content” link.
  • Pages use proper headings, landmarks and labelled form fields, so screen readers can announce them.
  • Photographs have text descriptions; purely decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
  • Text and controls are designed for readable contrast and can be enlarged with the browser zoom (up to 200%) without losing content.
  • The layout adapts to phones and tablets.
  • A “Pause motion” button in the Contact section stops the background film and all other movement. If your device is set to “reduce motion”, they never start.
  • The menu that stays at the bottom of the screen has a solid backing, so it never covers other text with its own.
  • The website works in current versions of Chrome, Safari, Firefox and Edge, on computers and phones.

7.2 Known limitations

  • The main website is in English. These legal notes are available in English and in Hebrew (עברית).
  • Some portfolio photographs may have only a general description (for example, “photograph 3 of 12”) rather than a detailed one. Descriptions are being improved.
  • External services linked from this website (Instagram, WhatsApp, Gmail) are not under my control.
